# Close-up round: shared rules (2026-10-31)

The user asked for a second massive detail wave, after the baking session. In their words, the person view and any close view
matter as much as the far view, "a tiny bit" more, with the goal of both. They wander and sample the city, so a hand-designed
shop they may never enter is wasted: **everything must be cross-cutting**. They approved ten sessions and are away for 36 hours.

**Nobody will answer a question.** Never wait for a person. On a decision you would have asked about, take the conservative
choice, put it behind a toggle, write it in your log and carry on. The coordinator session wakes you, merges and reports.

Read before starting: this file, `CLAUDE.md`, the top of `docs/progress/LEAD.md`, `docs/progress/baking*.md` (what the baking

## What counts
- Judge from walking (`V`) and from zoom at about 1, 3 and 10 m, at noon, 18.2 and 21.5, **sampled across ordinary places all over
  the city**, not the pretty corners. A flaw visible only up close is now a flaw; "nobody stands that near" is no longer a reason.
- Build **systems that reach every building, person or street**. No hand-built one-off places or hero pieces, and no new rooms to walk into.
- Far views must not get worse, in look or in cost.
- Look for what reads as fake: a repeated module, a flat surface, a missing contact with the ground, something with no reason to be there.
- No sound work. No gameplay. Everything still comes from our own code: no external assets, scans or photographs. Baked results
  generated by our code are allowed, as the baking session established. No new runtime dependencies.
- People: start realistic-stylised (good proportions and character, like a quality animated film, not a photo). If budget remains,
  push towards realism step by step and stop where it starts to look strange. Judge in motion as well as in stills.

## Budget
Target: a steady 30 fps on the Adreno X1-45 at 1920×1025 on high. Baking's log has the current frame times and prices.
- **At most +0.2 ms at overview and downtown; at most +0.7 ms at street `5,1.5,-65,9,0,6` and market `27.5,1,78.5,16,200,28`**, per
  session, on the shared switch. Close detail lives only near the camera, so far views should barely notice it. Over budget means trim.
- 0 mid-run shader compiles and no freezes when near detail appears (`hitch.mjs`, and walk through a change of area), no
  per-frame allocations, console clean.
- Every feature has a master switch in your own `src/core/close<name>.ts` (style of `src/core/nature.ts`) and a quality tier.

Baking is merged (a81efd9); read `docs/progress/baking.md`, "Rules for anyone working after this". In short:
- **Run `npm run bake` in your worktree after `npm ci` and before any perf measurement**, and again after changing a shader or geometry a bake depends on. A missing or stale bake is silent and costs up to 7 ms. Use your worktree's own `npm run bake`, never the main checkout's script: `scripts/bake.mjs` writes next to itself.
- A switch that changes a shader chunk the facade bake depends on can lose the bake and read as +7 ms at far views (`?noclosel` did after stage 2). If your switch shows that, the switch is not a measure: compare against a copy of your build from before the feature.
- A new term that changes with the hour or the camera in `bhWall` must also go into `FAR_FRAG_MAIN` in `src/world/buildings/farFacade.ts`, or it stops at the hand-over at about 100 m.
- Frames at noon after the merge: overview 32.4, downtown 30.5, street 35.7, market 34.0 ms. **Baking gained nothing at street and market, where walls stand 15–25 m away (5–6 ms there), and these are the views this round cares about most.** Near-camera savings are as welcome as new detail: a session that finds one should take it and report it.
- Don't rely on `?pvs`: it is off because of GPU context losses.

## How to work
- When the start message comes, first bring your branch up to the coordinator's head: use the `sync_with_base_branch` tool, or
  `git merge --no-edit refs/heads/claude/pensive-gates-bim12i` if that is not available. Then `npm ci` and
  `npx vite --port <your port> --strictPort --host 127.0.0.1`.
- Run every harness script from the main checkout so that all sessions share one browser lock:
  `node C:/Projects/GameCity/scripts/<script>.mjs --url http://127.0.0.1:<port>/ …`. Keep captures in small batches; a run can wait
  over 10 minutes in the queue, so run them in the background. Always Read the PNGs.
- close-foundation's test set and bubble are the common tools: use them instead of writing your own.
- Work in rounds: audit, rank by wow per ms, build, verify by eye, measure, commit, log. At least three rounds, a fresh audit each.
  Keep going until nothing on your list is worth its cost or the coordinator's time limit arrives. Use subagents freely, under 10 at a time.
- Commit after every feature; keep `docs/progress/<session>.md` with a "Resume here" section after every milestone.
- Don't merge, push or deploy: the coordinator merges. Commits end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.
- Disk: keep `shots/` small, delete your old captures, and stop writing if the disk has under 5 GB free.

## Power (the charger contact is unreliable)
On battery, frame times double and look exactly like a regression. Use `scripts/power-guard.mjs` for every timing and discard numbers from a stopped
run. Captures for looking at images are fine on battery. If the battery is under 25 % and discharging, stop browser work, commit, log, and end your turn saying so.

## Quota
Check `mcp__ccd_session_mgmt__get_usage` regularly. If the weekly figure reaches 90 % or the 5-hour figure reaches 95 %, finish the change in
hand, commit, update the log, and end your turn with the word PAUSED-FOR-QUOTA: the coordinator wakes you after the reset.

## Final checks of a session
`npm run typecheck`, `npm run build`, `ui-test.mjs --q noidle` in full (`kinds` is a known flake: rerun it alone), `shot.mjs --view overview
--invariants --validate --speed 4 --wait 20000` with invariants `{}` and validate `[]`, `hitch.mjs` 0 mid-run compiles, `?diorama` and
`?quality=medium|low` still fine, perf on your switch. End your last message with the word CLOSE-DONE and a short report: what you added, toggles,
perf and triangle cost, the best view and hour to see it, what is left.
