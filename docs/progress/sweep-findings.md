## Top families, what the judges said

### Cracks, rust, chips and line artifacts read as flat decals stamped on surfaces (`decals-cracks-stains-pasted`)
- 4 bug: `objects/area-beach-sand/12_1m`: the dark grimy smudges at the bottom-left and the dirty halo along the towel edge. They look like soot or mould on the sand. The blurry blotches have no source.
- 4 fake: `objects/area-yard-concrete/12_3m.jpg`: the pale grey flakes and shards on the red container wall. They are tiny flat triangles and specks stuck on the surface. They read as pasted decals, not chipped paint, and they show no metal underneath. / 
- 4 fake: `objects/bldback-harbormaster/12_3m.jpg`: the rust streaks under every block joint. The streaks are the same width and length and repeat at even spacing. They read as a stamped pattern, not as real staining.

### Pavement, road, plaza and ground edges are flat, featureless or hard-edged with no wear or transition (`ground-surfaces-flat`)
- 5 fake: `objects/area-playground-sand/12_1m.jpg`: the whole ground, a beige slab floor with no sand on it. The brief says sand. This is flat grey-beige paving with the same fine grain everywhere, with no loose grains, ripples, footprints or drifts.
- 5 bug: `objects/crossing-1/12_3m.jpg`: the grey concrete crossing slab over the rails. It looks tilted and floating above the road. The near edge rises off the asphalt and the right end overhangs the kerb and pavement, so it doesn't sit in the road. / 
- 4 bug: `objects/area-boardwalk-deck/12_10m.jpg`: the foreground deck planks at the bottom right. The planks are a different scale and a saturated orange, with no grey weathering. They don't match the same deck on the left, so it reads like a different material.

### Textures are smeared, stretched, low-res or mis-assigned (`textures-smeared-stretched`)
- 5 bug: `objects/feat-breakwater/12_1m.jpg`: the whole frame, with smeared shards of dark rock and pale fragments. The view is a mess of overlapping translucent shards with jagged cut-off edges. Geometry passes through the water surface, and nothing in it can be identified.
- 4 fake: `objects/area-yard-concrete/12_3m.jpg`: the container wall's wood-like grain. It has a horizontal wood grain with plank strips, but it should be steel. The material doesn't match what the object is. / 
- 4 bug: `objects/bench-path/12_1m.jpg`: the left armrest. It is pale grey or white while the right armrest is dark charcoal. The two sides don't match, and the left one looks like a wrong material. / 

### Street-level and interior lighting is dusk-dark at noon and doesn't match other views (`lighting-inconsistent-noon`)
- 5 bug: `objects/bldback-house/12_10m.jpg`: the sky behind the house. The sky is dusky blue with visible stars, which doesn't match noon. It also doesn't match the lighting on the roof.
- 5 bug: `objects/cs-text-aboard/12_1m.jpg`: the whole close-up scene. It's almost black with a blue night tint, but the 3 m shot shows full noon sun. The lighting and shadows are wrong at close range. / 
- 5 fake: `objects/cs-wall-warehouse/12_1m.jpg`: the whole close view. At "noon" the picture is murky and dark, with a dim blue-grey cast. It looks like night or a dark filter laid over the wall, and the sun is nowhere to be seen. / 

### White (or red) dots hang in the sky and in front of facades like dead pixels or daytime stars (`sky-specks-dots`)
- 5 bug: `objects/bld-balcony/12_1m.jpg`: the small red dots and white specks floating in the sky and near the balconies. They hang in mid-air like debris, and some sit right on the railing. They read as stray particles.
- 4 fake: `objects/bld-apartment-residential-flat-plain/12_up.jpg`: the white dots all over the sky and in front of the facade. They are bright, sharp specks like snow or stars. They would not appear at noon and look like dust on the lens. / 
- 4 bug: `objects/bld-house-roof-halfhip/12_1m.jpg`: White star dots scattered across the blue noon sky.. Stars are visible in full daylight, which is wrong for noon. They look like floating specks.

### Door handles, closers, lamps, hinge straps and shutter hardware float with no mounts or brackets (`fittings-floating-unattached`)
- 5 bug: `objects/cs-text-tenants/12_1m.jpg`: The black DIRECTORY board hangs in the air inside the shop window, with no frame, posts or fixings. It's a flat slab with no wall or glass behind it to hold it. It sits inside the pharmacy, so a list of upstairs offices is in the wrong place.
- 4 bug: `objects/bld-church-oldtown-gable-plain/12_3m`: the two open church doors. They are thin, twisted slivers with an X of black straps. The straps run past the wood and cross each other in mid-air. The doors read as broken planes, not as heavy wooden leaves.
- 4 bug: `objects/bld-house-plan-bay/12_1m.jpg`: the white front door has a black lever handle and a brass knob. The knob sits on the dark frame, to the right of the door leaf. A second handle on the frame is impossible, and the leaf has no hinges where it would open.

### People are stiff low-poly mannequins with blank faces, pasted onto the ground (`people-mannequin-stiff-blank`)
- 5 bug: `objects/feat-fountain/12_3m.jpg`: the person in the blue helmet sitting on the fountain rim. The legs hang down in front of the outer wall, with no thickness and no contact. The purple bag and its handle stick out into the water and hang in mid-air.
- 4 fake: `objects/area-market-paving/12_10m`: the pale woman and the other shoppers in the foreground. Their skin is a glossy blank white with no face detail, so they look like shop mannequins. Their limbs are thin and stiff. / 
- 4 bug: `objects/bld-house-plan-veranda/12_3m.jpg`: the woman with the red bag beside the porch rail. Her body and bag overlap the corner post and the railing, so she seems to stand half inside the porch. Her feet have no clear footing on the steps.

- 5 bug: `objects/cs-walk-home2/12_10m.jpg`: the big pale-blue curved sheet in the lower left, in front of the tree. It reads as a smeared, see-through ribbon with no clear purpose, and it blocks a fifth of the view. It looks like a camera-side object, or a shelter or bench with no thickness, the wrong colour and badly shaped. /
