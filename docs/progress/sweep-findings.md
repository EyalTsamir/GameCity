**The square-on fault distorts the ranking below.** Until the capture changed on 2026-11-02, pictures faced walls exactly square (344 kinds, and all cells look along axis-aligned streets). Within a few degrees of square-on, walls and panes lose their colour and interior. The same shop at 8° off is peach and lilac with a full interior, instead of pale with milky glass (`shots/sweep/objects/bld-townhouse-oldtown-flat-shop/12_10m.jpg` against `shots/sweep-check/off8/objects/bld-townhouse-oldtown-flat-shop/12_10m.jpg`). So glass, wall, lighting and blank-panel scores are all inflated for kinds shot so far. From now on the capture stands 8° off. Those kinds get shot and judged again after the fault is fixed.

**Ready for a fix session, in this order:**
1. `wall-material-differs-by-distance`: the square-on fault. A real bug, reproduced, and it matters most because it also hides other flaws. A walker sees a wall flash when looking straight at it.
2. `sky-specks-dots`: white specks at noon in the sky and in front of facades. Seen in most of the pictures I opened. Known item, probably cheap.
3. `decals-cracks-stains-pasted`: black squiggle cracks on plaster at 3 m, and the same squiggles on harbour rock.
4. `people-mannequin-stiff-blank`: close up, hair strips and hat brims turn into huge spikes, and faces are crude. Large job.

## Top families, what the judges said

### People are stiff low-poly mannequins with blank faces, pasted onto the ground (`people-mannequin-stiff-blank`)
- 5: `objects/bld-church-oldtown-gable-plain/12_1m.jpg`: The two people's heads filling the foreground. Faces and hair are crude low-poly with a painted-on hair parting stripe. The dark-haired head is semi-transparent with a dithered, see-through patch. They float at the camera, cropped and blurred, with nothing to explain why they are there.
- 5: `objects/bld-harbormaster-harbor-hip-plain/12_3m.jpg`: The hat and hair of the pedestrian closest to the camera. The hat brim, hair and ribbon strands are huge flat spikes that cross half the frame. They read as broken geometry, not a hat and hair.
- 5: `objects/bld-kiosk-beach-flat-shop/12_1m.jpg`: The vendor behind the counter. The head is a dark, faceless ball with a white cap, and it floats above a boxy white torso with a pink apron. The neck is missing, there are no arms or hands to speak of, and the figure is flat and unlit. Up close it reads as a mannequin made of blocks.

### Small props and furniture are crude low-poly blobs or identical flat blocks (`props-lowpoly-blobs`)
- 5: `objects/cs-furn-bench/12_1m.jpg`: Bench slats and the front rail. They are smooth, flat green boxes with no wood grain, bolts or rounded edges. They look like plastic, and the faint pale chips look like decals. The slats also seem to float, with no visible fixings to the frame.
- 5: `objects/cs-furn-bin/12_1m.jpg`: Flat grey pyramid lid with a black square slab on top. The lid is one smooth, evenly lit grey surface with no seams, rivets, handle or opening. The black square looks like a placeholder pasted on, not a real feature. The four facets show only a faint shading change. / 
- 5: `objects/cs-ground-plaza/12_1m.jpg`: The pigeons: low-poly, faceted grey bodies with flat shading, no feathers, eyes, legs or feet, and spiky ear-like head tips. They look like folded paper. Seen from 1 m, they look like cut-out fish or lozenges rather than birds.
